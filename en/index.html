About

Who you'd be working with

Sluggan AB is me — Eric Österberg, a software engineer based in Uppsala.

I've spent the last few years working as a professional developer, mostly backend systems in C#/.NET and Java, and full-stack web work with React and TypeScript. Day to day that's meant things like indoor positioning systems and API integrations for the security industry, but I've built plenty of smaller things on the side too.

I started Sluggan AB because a lot of small businesses end up either going without a proper website or app, or paying agency prices for something a lot simpler than what they actually need. I'd rather just build the thing you need, talk to you directly while I'm doing it, and charge a fair, fixed price for it.

  • I agree on a price before I start, so nothing changes on you later.

  • This is my actual profession, not a side project I'm learning on.

Questions

Things people usually ask

If yours isn't here, that's what the contact form is for.

What does it cost?

Depends entirely on scope. A simple website is a lot cheaper than a custom app with logins and a backend. Get in touch and you'll get a free quote with a fixed price, before anything gets built.

How long does it take?

A straightforward website is often a couple of weeks. Something with a backend, accounts, or a mobile app takes longer. I'll give you a realistic timeline as part of the plan, and you'll hear from me along the way rather than finding out at the end.

Do you build for both iOS and Android?

Yes. Depending on the project I'll use React Native or similar cross-platform tools so one codebase covers both, or go native if that's genuinely the better fit. I'll walk you through the trade-off before we start.

I don't have a clear idea yet, is that a problem?

Not at all. Most projects start as a rough idea. We'll talk it through, and if it makes sense I'll help you shape it into something concrete before any code gets written.

Can you take over or improve something that already exists?

Yes — a website or app someone else built, one you built yourself, or something that's stalled halfway through. I'll take a look and tell you honestly what state it's in and what it'd take to move forward.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Work is scoped and priced per project, not as an ongoing retainer. If you only need one thing built, that's the whole engagement.

What if it's just a small job, like one page?

That's fine. Small and one-off jobs are welcome, not just big builds — see the About section for more on that.
